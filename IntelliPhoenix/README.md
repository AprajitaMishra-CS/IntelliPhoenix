# IntelliPhoenix

### Where Intelligence Inspires Recovery

IntelliPhoenix is an AI-powered healthcare management web application designed to make healthcare assistance more accessible, organized, and user-friendly.

The application combines AI-assisted symptom analysis with healthcare management features such as doctor recommendations, appointment booking, digital health reports, notifications, and emergency support.

> Disclaimer: IntelliPhoenix provides AI-assisted informational guidance only. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or consultation.

## About the Project

Traditional healthcare management can involve difficulties such as manual appointment handling, scattered health information, and limited access to preliminary healthcare guidance.

IntelliPhoenix aims to provide a centralized digital platform where users can manage healthcare-related activities and receive preliminary AI-assisted information based on the symptoms they provide.

The system is designed to improve accessibility, organization, and convenience while keeping professional medical consultation as the final authority.

## Key Features

### AI Symptom Analysis
Users can enter their symptoms and receive AI-assisted information about possible health concerns.

The system may provide:
- Possible health conditions
- Severity-level information
- Basic precautions
- Recommended medical specialists

The results are informational and should not be considered a confirmed medical diagnosis.

### Doctor Recommendation
The system can suggest an appropriate medical specialist based on the user's symptoms and healthcare requirements.

### Appointment Booking
Users can:
- Search for available doctors
- View doctor information
- Book appointments
- Manage scheduled appointments

### Digital Health Reports
Users can access and manage their healthcare-related reports digitally.

### In-App Notifications
The application provides notifications for important healthcare activities such as appointment-related updates.

### Health Dashboard
The dashboard provides users with a centralized view of important healthcare information including appointments and reports.

### AI Health Assistance
Users can interact with an AI-based healthcare assistance feature for general healthcare-related questions.

### Emergency Support
Provides quick access to emergency-related contact information.

###  Authentication
The system provides secure user registration and login functionality.

Different roles can be supported, such as:

- Patient
- Doctor
- Administrator

## User Roles

### Patient
Patients can:
- Register and login
- Enter symptoms for AI-assisted analysis
- Receive preliminary health information
- View recommended specialists
- Book appointments
- Manage appointments
- Access health reports
- Receive notifications
- Use AI healthcare assistance

### Doctor
Doctors can manage their professional information and appointments and interact with patient-related healthcare activities according to their permissions.

### Administrator
Administrators can manage system-level information, users, doctors, appointments, and other application resources.
